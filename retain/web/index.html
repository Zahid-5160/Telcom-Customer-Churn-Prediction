Fairness check — attrition by gender

Reported so it can be monitored. Gender is deliberately never used by the model and never appears in a recommendation.

Why this sits apart. Training on a protected characteristic would let the model learn who leaves by sex and then price retention offers accordingly — unlawful in most places and wrong everywhere. Watching for an imbalance is a different job from predicting with it, and only the first one belongs here.
